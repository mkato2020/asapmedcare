How We Work

Source. Register. Distribute.

Three steps, run in sequence, with the same team accountable from the manufacturer’s factory gate to the dispensing counter.

  1. Step 01

    Source

    We partner directly with manufacturers — no broker layers — and audit quality systems, GMP status and supply reliability before we commit to a market.

  2. Step 02

    Register

    We prepare and carry the National Drug Authority dossier ourselves, holding the registration, the import permits and the post-market obligations that follow it.

  3. Step 03

    Distribute

    Stock reaches pharmacies, hospitals and clinics with clinical detailing attached, so the prescriber understands the product as well as the buyer does.

Quality & Compliance

What a regulator, a hospital and a manufacturer each need to see.

Distribution is a licensed activity, not a logistics one. These are the obligations we hold ourselves to on every line we carry — and the records we can produce on request.

Market authorisation

We hold the National Drug Authority registration for every product we import, and we keep it current. The registration sits with us — not with an agent you cannot reach.

Storage & cold chain

Stock is held under the conditions its dossier specifies, with temperature monitoring and a documented procedure for excursions. Cold chain lines are handled as cold chain lines.

Batch traceability

Every consignment is traceable to batch and expiry — from the manufacturer's release certificate through import clearance to the delivery note signed at your door.

Pharmacovigilance

Adverse event reports are captured, investigated and forwarded to both the National Drug Authority and the manufacturer. Reporting is a condition of carrying a product, not a favour.

Complaints & recall

A product complaint reaches a named person the same working day. Because we hold batch records end to end, a recall can be scoped to the affected consignment rather than the market.

Documentation on request

Certificates of analysis, batch release documents, import permits and storage records — assembled for tender submissions and audits without a three-week wait.

Questions

Asked before every first order.

If your procurement team needs something not covered here, ask us directly — a vague answer at this stage is a problem later.

Are your products registered with the National Drug Authority?

Yes. We do not supply a pharmaceutical product into the Ugandan market before its registration is in place, and we hold that registration ourselves. Where a product is mid-registration we will say so plainly and give you the expected timeline rather than an optimistic one.

Can you provide documentation for a tender submission?

Certificates of analysis, batch release documentation, import permits, registration certificates and storage records. Tell us the tender deadline when you ask — assembling a complete pack takes us days, not weeks, but it is not instant.

What are your lead times?

Lines held in stock in Kampala ship on the normal delivery cycle. Products ordered against a forecast depend on the manufacturer's production and shipping schedule, and we will give you that date in writing before you commit. We would rather quote a longer date and meet it.

Do you handle cold chain products?

Yes, under monitored conditions with documented excursion handling. If a consignment breaches its temperature range in transit, it does not get quietly delivered — it is quarantined and investigated.

Do you supply outside Kampala?

We supply across Uganda, with delivery frequency to upcountry facilities agreed as part of the supply terms rather than promised in general. Regional East African supply is being built deliberately, market by market, as registrations allow.

We are a manufacturer. What do you need from us to start?

The product dossier, your GMP certification and manufacturing licence, available clinical and comparator data, and an indication of supply capacity. We review before we discuss commercial terms, because the evidence decides whether there are terms to discuss.
